Technology 1: Computers

Computers operate through a rapid pulsing of electrical currents. The core foundation of computers is binary code, which determines whether there is an electrical current. The machine codes in the form of binary instructions, a combination of zeroes and ones, prompting the computer to perform various functions, including arithmetic calculations, logical operations, image processing and language translation. Computers are vital in storing and retrieving information, performing complex mathematical and scientific calculations, and executing software. Apart from simpler tasks, they’re also used in machine learning applications, big data analytics, and robotic and automation tasks.

Technology 2: The Internet

Many people consider the internet the most popular and powerful communication technology. It allows people from around the world to interact through written messages and audio and video messages in real-time. The internet is a powerful tool for businesses, charities, governments and individuals to use for marketing, communication and outreach, information broadcasting, social interactions and information sharing.
